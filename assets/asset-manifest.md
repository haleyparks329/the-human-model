# Asset Manifest

This manifest records provenance and publication history for committed visual assets. It does not make an evergreen publication decision: every future reuse or promotion requires a fresh review of visible content, metadata, consent, and context.

## Policy

- Private health exports, raw workspace exports, credentials, local paths, and private automation files do not belong in this repository.
- Personal metrics, chat content, and training data must be synthetic, redacted, or explicitly approved for the specific publication.
- Metadata must be reviewed before publication.
- Approval recorded on 2026-07-13 was date-bounded. It is historical evidence, not automatic approval for a new use.
- Screenshots demonstrate historical interfaces. They do not prove current system behavior or architecture.
