# Research Notes

The Human Model studies how software can represent a changing person as longitudinal, provenance-aware, reviewable state. Bodybuilding and training supplied the first demanding test environment; they are the origin, not the limit, of the research.

## Current working questions

### Representation

- What belongs in canonical human state rather than in a source record, product cache, or specialist interpretation?
- How should observations, inferences, and confirmations remain distinguishable over time?
- What is the smallest ontology that supports claims, events, plans, schedules, preferences, routines, intents, proposals, corrections, projections, and evaluations without flattening their differences?

### Time and uncertainty

- When does evidence become stale, and who is allowed to revalidate it?
- How should contradictory sources be preserved and resolved?
- Where are confidence values useful, and where would they manufacture precision?

### Human control

- Can a person inspect why a claim exists, correct it, and see the downstream effect?
- Which changes require explicit confirmation?
- How can repeated friction or workarounds reveal a desire path without becoming covert behavioral profiling?

### Product boundaries

- What minimum-sufficient projection does each resident need?
- How can Coach remain a specialist interpreter while the Human Model remains the owner of accepted state?
- How can Bridget coordinate continuity without becoming a mandatory gateway or a second Human Model?

### Evaluation

- Does a modeled signal improve a real decision compared with simpler evidence?
- Can the system explain failures, missing data, and reversals?
- Does performance hold longitudinally, across changing routines and eventually across people and domains?

## Evidence discipline

The current private system provides evidence for a bounded performance model, proposal workflows, plan/schedule handling, and resident projections. It does not yet establish generalized intent learning, universal confidence decay, broad autonomy, or a complete model of a person.

Historical Telegram, dashboard, readiness, and movement prototypes are useful interface and research lineage. They are not evidence that those surfaces or algorithms define the current architecture.

Public work should prefer synthetic fixtures and narrow reference implementations. Private personal records, prompts, thresholds, schemas, and cross-system operational contracts remain out of scope.
