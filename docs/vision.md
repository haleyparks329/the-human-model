# Vision

The Human Model aims to make long-running human context dependable without making it totalizing.

In the desired future, a person can move between specialist tools and residents without repeatedly rebuilding context. Each consumer receives a bounded projection appropriate to its job. Observations keep their sources, interpretations remain reviewable, accepted state has a clear owner, and corrections improve future behavior without erasing history.

The bodybuilding and performance workflow remains the primary proving ground. It supplies concrete questions about plans, training, nutrition, recovery, measurements, interventions, and outcomes. The architecture should generalize only where evidence supports it.

## What success looks like

- A person can inspect why an important claim exists.
- Conflicting and missing evidence remains visible.
- Proposed actions are separated from confirmed canonical changes.
- Accepted plans and later supersessions have a traceable history.
- Specialist systems work from minimum-sufficient projections.
- Repeated correction and workflow friction can improve the system without being mistaken for consent or fact.
- Interfaces can change without losing the underlying model.

## What success does not mean

- a complete digital replica of a person;
- a chatbot that owns all memory;
- automatic inference presented as truth;
- a dashboard treated as the product;
- autonomous high-impact action;
- publishing private schemas, prompts, thresholds, or personal data to prove that the architecture exists.

The north star is responsible continuity: useful state across time, explicit uncertainty, and preserved human authority.
